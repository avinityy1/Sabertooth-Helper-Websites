Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Sabertooth Helper is a private utility bot built exclusively for managing our restricted Google Sheet. Strictly for the use of Sabertooth Squad in the 104th battalion of Vikinglaw's Roblox GAR.

1. Data We Collect

  • Discord User ID, Server Nickname, and Squad Roles
  • Public Roblox User ID and Roblox Username
  • Selected Timezone (GMT/EST) and Squad Event Attendance

2. How Data Is Stored & Used

This data is used strictly to verify squad membership and track attendance on our restricted Google Sheet. Data is never sold or shared with outside third parties.

3. Data Removal

To request removal of your linked data, contact server staff or the bot developer via Discord.
